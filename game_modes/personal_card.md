# Personal Card

More of a feature than a game mode, but here's some important things to know.

## Menu location

On PC press escape or use the icon in the top right corner (3 hexagons):

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/25a45556-a930-4ef1-9726-2f4d6d4340a0)

## Personal Card tabs

Similar to Sequential phantasm, there is a very easy to miss tab menu on the top right of the `Personal Card` page when editing your card to change the outer border for it.

Within editing mode, click on `Personal Card` then look in the top right of the page

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/06028c30-d2d9-4162-bd75-f51d1501330d)
