# Vehicles
There are a variety of vehicles/mounts in the game which include:

- road vehicles
- underwater vehicles
- mecha
- weapon mounts (Nola, Gray Fox)
- relics (the surf board)

# Speed buffs
There are at least a couple of ways to increase vehicle speed:

- buy a Pay to win vehicle
- activate Hilda trait
